\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Working memory}} \\*

N-Back
& N-back task~\cite{R1}
& accuracy, reaction time, missed and false responses

Spatial Span

Letter--Number Sequencing
& Letter--number sequencing~\cite{R2}

Operation Span

& 10 levels: larger sets and more difficult arithmetic operations. \\

Dual N-Back$^\dagger$
& Dual n-back~\cite{R5}
& position accuracy, letter accuracy, combined accuracy, score
& 10 levels: 1- to 3-back, longer streams, and faster presentation. \\

\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Processing speed}} \\*

Simple Reaction Time
& Simple reaction time~\cite{R6}
& average reaction time, variability, choice accuracy, score
& 10 levels: changes waiting time and number of response choices. \\

Symbol--Digit Matching
& Symbol Digit Modalities Test~\cite{R7}
& correct and incorrect matches, completion time
& 10 levels: more symbols and more items. \\

Numeric Trail
& Trail Making Test, part A~\cite{R8}
& completion time, errors, score
& 10 levels: more nodes and more difficult layouts. \\

Pattern Comparison
& Pattern comparison~\cite{R9}
& accuracy, reaction time, timeouts, score
& 10 levels: larger and more similar patterns. \\

Inspection Time
& Inspection time~\cite{R10}
& accuracy, shortest correctly identified display time, score
& 10 levels: shorter presentation time and smaller differences between lines. \\

Choice Reaction Time$^\dagger$
& Choice reaction time~\cite{R11}
& accuracy, reaction time, consistency, score
& 10 levels: 2 to 4 choices and shorter response windows. Level increases if score is at least 83 and accuracy at least 82\%; it decreases if score is below 63. \\

\newpage
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Attention}} \\*

Continuous Performance
& Continuous performance test~\cite{R12}
& hit rate, false responses, reaction time, score
& Baseline: 60 trials. In training, 10 trials are added for each level above 3. \\

Serial Addition
& Paced serial addition (PASAT)~\cite{R13}
& correct answers, omissions, reaction time
& 10 levels: shorter intervals between presented digits. \\

Color--Word Interference
& Stroop test~\cite{R14}
& accuracy, interference effect, reaction time
& 10 levels: more colors, more incongruent trials, and shorter response time. \\

Go/No-Go
& Go/no-go task~\cite{R30,R31}
& accuracy on go and no-go trials, reaction time, consistency
& 10 levels: greater stimulus similarity, more no-go trials, and shorter response time. \\

Flanker
& Flanker task~\cite{R15}
& accuracy, interference effect, reaction time
& 10 levels: more incongruent trials and reduced spacing between stimuli. \\

Sustained-Attention Response$^\dagger$
& Sustained attention to response task~\cite{R16}
& accuracy, commission errors, reaction time
& 10 levels: longer streams and faster presentation. Level increases if score is at least 84 with at most 4 commission errors; it decreases if score is below 64. \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Flexibility}} \\*

Task Switching
& Cued task switching~\cite{R17}
& accuracy, switch cost, perseverative errors
& Baseline: 40 trials. In training, 10 trials are added for each level above 4. \\

Alternating Trail
& Trail Making Test, part B~\cite{R8}
& completion time, errors, difference from part A
& 10 levels: more nodes and more difficult layouts. \\

Card-Sorting Rule Shift
& Wisconsin Card Sorting Test~\cite{R18}
& categories completed, perseverative errors, accuracy
& 10 levels: more trials and more frequent rule changes. \\

Dimensional Card Sort
& Dimensional Change Card Sort~\cite{R19}
& accuracy, reaction time, switch cost
& 10 levels: more rule changes and shorter response time. \\

Rule Shift$^\dagger$
& Cued set shifting~\cite{R17}
& overall accuracy, switch accuracy, reaction time, perseverative errors
& 10 levels: more rules and faster cues. Level increases if score is at least 83, switch accuracy at least 72\%, and perseverative errors at most 5; it decreases if score is below 63. \\

Plus--Minus Switching
& Jersild switching task~\cite{R20}
& accuracy, reaction time, switch cost
& 10 levels: larger numbers and more mixed blocks. \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Planning}} \\*

Tower Planning
& Tower of London~\cite{R21}
& problems solved, additional moves, time, score
& 10 levels: more problems and more difficult configurations. \\

Stockings-Style Planning
& Stockings of Cambridge~\cite{R22}
& problems solved, additional moves, time, score
& 10 levels: more balls and more difficult problems. \\

Letter Fluency
& Phonemic fluency~\cite{R23}
& number of valid unique words, errors
& 10 levels: different letter sets and increasing prompt requirements. \\

Category Fluency$^\dagger$
& Semantic fluency~\cite{R23}
& number of valid unique words, repeated words
& 10 levels: different categories with increasing expected word counts. \\

Twenty Questions$^\dagger$
& Twenty Questions~\cite{R24}
& successful identification, questions used, strategy score
& 10 levels: different object sets with increasing difficulty. \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Visual scanning}} \\*

Visual Search$^\dagger$
& Visual search~\cite{R25}
& targets found, accuracy, time per target
& 10 levels: more items and greater similarity between targets and distractors. \\

Cancellation$^\dagger$
& Cancellation test~\cite{R26}
& hits, omissions, incorrect selections, time
& 10 levels: grids of 150 to 540 cells with increasing target requirements. \\

Feature and Conjunction Search
& Feature-integration search~\cite{R25}
& accuracy, search time
& 10 levels: larger sets and more similar distractors. \\

Landmark Line Judgment$^\dagger$
& Line bisection~\cite{R27}
& accuracy, omissions, spatial bias, reaction time
& 10 levels: smaller offsets and shorter presentation. Level increases if score is at least 84, absolute bias is at most 8, and omissions are at most 1; it decreases if score is below 64. \\

Multiple-Object Tracking$^\dagger$
& Multiple-object tracking~\cite{R28}
& accuracy, correct and incorrect selections
& 10 levels: 6 to 16 objects, 1 to 3 targets, and increasing movement speed. \\

Central--Peripheral Attention
& Useful field of view~\cite{R29}
& central-target and peripheral-target accuracy
& Levels 1--3 use a central target only; levels 4--6 add a peripheral target; levels 7--10 add distractors. Presentation time decreases from 800 to 150 ms. \\

\end{longtable}

\noindent\textit{Note.}
All tasks have automated scoring tests; tasks marked $^\dagger$ also have
tests of trial generation or difficulty adjustment. These tests check the
software, not psychometric or clinical validity, which has not been
established. Trial structure, stimuli, timing, scoring, and difficulty
progression are specific to NeuroBloom.

\caption{Session-planning and difficulty-adaptation settings used in
Algorithm~1 of the main text.}
\label{tab:planning}\\

\toprule
Parameter & Setting \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead

\toprule
Parameter & Setting \\
\midrule
\endhead

Initial difficulty
& Assigned once when a new training plan is created from the domain baseline
score: $<50$, level 2; 50--69, level 4; 70--84, level 7;
$\geq85$, level 9. \\

Generic submission pathway: timing
& Applied once all four tasks of a session are completed, using task accuracy
only. \\

Generic submission pathway: difficulty increase
& Increased by one level, up to level 10, when task accuracy in a completed
session is at least 85\%. \\

Generic submission pathway: difficulty decrease
& Decreased by one level, down to level 1, when task accuracy is below 65\%.
The level remains unchanged for accuracy from 65\% to below 85\%. \\

Task-specific modules
& Applied after each task, using that task's own performance measures and
thresholds. Difficulty may increase or decrease by one or two levels,
within the range of 1--10. \\

Focus rebalancing
& Reconsidered after every fourth completed session. All six domains are
ranked using each domain's mean score among the 12 most recent task results,
or its baseline score when it has no recent result. The two lowest-ranked
domains become primary focus areas, the next two secondary focus areas and
the two highest maintenance domains. \\

Clinician-selected focus areas
& Kept unchanged; automatic rebalancing does not overwrite focus areas set by
a clinician for the current training plan. Automatic rebalancing resumes when
a new training plan is created from a new baseline. \\

Tasks per session
& Four tasks are selected: two from primary-focus domains and two from
secondary-focus domains. \\

\bottomrule

\end{longtable}

\noindent\textit{Note.}
All values were chosen by the developers and have not been empirically
optimized. Four tasks per session allows two primary-focus and two
secondary-focus domains in each session. Partially completed sessions do not
count toward rebalancing. If recent tasks cannot be excluded, selection falls
back to the full domain task pool and tasks may repeat. Tied domain scores
follow a fixed order: working memory, attention, cognitive flexibility,
planning, processing speed, and visual scanning.

Fatigue-proxy weights
(accuracy, RT, slope)
& 0.5, 0.3, 0.2
& Equal weighting; accuracy-weighted; RT-weighted
& The proxy changed by at most 0.03, 0.10, and 0.15, respectively, on a

Fatigue-proxy normalization constants
& 30 percentage points, 200 ms, 0.01 per trial
& All three constants scaled together by $\pm20\%$
& The proxy changed by at most 0.04 ($-20\%$) and 0.05 ($+20\%$). The

EWMA smoothing constant $\alpha$
& 0.2
& 0.1, 0.3, 0.5
& The trend label changed in 2, 1, and 1 of the 5 synthetic series,
respectively. \\

Trend window
& 5 points
& 3 and 7 points
& The trend label changed in 1 of 5 series with a three-point window and in
0 of 5 series with a seven-point window. \\

Trend classification threshold
& $\pm1\%$ relative slope
& $\pm0.5\%$ and $\pm2\%$
& The trend label changed in 0 and 3 of the 5 series, respectively.
Gradual trends near the decision boundary were most affected. \\

Minimum data requirements
& 8 valid trials for the fatigue proxy; 3 valid scores for WPSC; 3 valid
observations for EWMA trend classification
& Not varied
& Insufficient data is reported when the corresponding minimum is not met.
Available EWMA values are retained without a trend label. \\

Review-flag thresholds
& 14-day review-flag score: baseline $<45$ (+2); recent mean 6--12 points below
baseline (+1) or $\geq12$ below (+2); recent mean $<50$ (+1); fatigue
$\geq6.5$ (+1) or $\geq8$ (+2); completion $<60\%$ with $\geq3$ sessions
(+1). Total $\geq4$ high-priority, 2--3 moderate-priority review flag.
& Not varied
& Lower cut-offs would flag more patients. The thresholds are fixed during
normal use and can be changed by developers or deployers. \\

\end{longtable}

\noindent\textit{Note.}
Each parameter group was varied separately on synthetic data (five
40-trial sessions for the fatigue proxy; five 20-session score series for the
trend). The results show how the indicators respond to these settings, not
which values are clinically appropriate. The display bands for the fatigue
proxy (0.3, 0.5) and CV (0.25, 0.35) are not clinical thresholds; missing
indicators are not assigned a band.
